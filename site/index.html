The whole post, in one image

Everything embedded comes along

A screenshot cuts things off. Tweet Picker redraws the post from X's own data, so the image holds everything the post holds:

  • The full text, never cut at "Show more"
  • Every photo, uncropped
  • The quoted post, and the post it quotes
  • Link previews and X Articles in full
  • Counts and the date, in your X theme

No scrolling, no flashing: the page stays still while the image is made.

Threads too

A whole thread, in one image

On a post that's part of a thread, the same picture icon copies the entire thread: every post the author wrote in the chain, in order, on one card.

  • Click any post in the thread, first, middle or last
  • Posts not loaded on the page yet are fetched for you
  • Replies from other people are left out
  • Each post keeps its photos, quotes and full links

Links you can read

Full URLs, not site.com/pa…

X shortens long links to something like site.com/pa…, which is useless once the post becomes a picture. Tweet Picker writes every link out in full, so whoever sees the image knows exactly where it goes.

Link previews get their full address too, written just above the preview.

How to use

Hover what you want. The button is right there.

No setup: install it, open x.com, and point at a photo, a video or a post. Tabs that are already open work without a reload.

Copy a photo

Button in the photo's top-right corner. Then paste anywhere.

Save a video or GIF

Button in the video's top-right corner. The MP4 lands in Downloads.

Share a post as an image

Picture icon, first in the post's top-right icons. The image is on your clipboard. In a thread, it copies the whole thread.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do I download a video from Twitter / X?

Install Tweet Picker, open the post on x.com and hover the video or GIF. Click the download button in its top-right corner and the best-quality MP4 is saved to your Downloads folder.

How do I save a tweet as an image?

Hover the post and click the picture icon, first among the icons at its top right. Tweet Picker draws the whole tweet, including long text, every photo, quoted posts and link previews, as one image and copies it to your clipboard, ready to paste into a chat, document or slide.

How do I save a whole Twitter / X thread as an image?

Hover any post in the thread and click the same picture icon. Tweet Picker finds every post the author wrote in the chain, loads the ones the page hasn't shown yet, and draws them all in order on one image, without other people's replies.

How do I copy an image from a tweet?

Hover the photo and click the copy button in its corner. You get the original-size image on your clipboard, also from X's full-screen photo viewer.

Is Tweet Picker free? Does it need my X password?

It's free, with no account and no sign-in of its own. It works inside the X page you already have open and only talks to X's own servers.

Does it work on twitter.com as well as x.com?

Yes, on both, in light, dim and dark themes. It runs in Google Chrome and other Chromium browsers that install from the Chrome Web Store.
