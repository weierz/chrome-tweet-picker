The whole post, in one image

Everything embedded comes along

A screenshot cuts things off. TweetPicker redraws the post from X's own data, so the image holds everything the post holds:

  • The full text, never cut at "Show more"
  • Every photo, uncropped
  • The quoted post, and the post it quotes
  • Link previews and X Articles in full
  • Counts and the date, in your X theme

No scrolling, no flashing: the page stays still while the image is made.

How to use

Hover, click, paste

1

Install

Add TweetPicker to Chrome and pin it. x.com tabs that are already open work right away, no reload.

2

Copy a photo

Hover a photo and click in its top-right corner. Paste anywhere.

3

Save a video

Hover a video or GIF and click . The MP4 goes to your Downloads folder.

4

Share a post

Hover a post and click , the first of its top-right icons. The image is on your clipboard.

Click the toolbar icon for the options, all off by default: Like or Bookmark a post when you copy or share it, and Expand "Show more". A green check means it worked; a red ✕ shows why it didn't when you hover it.

Privacy

Stays in your browser

TweetPicker only talks to X's own servers, the same way the X page does. No account, no analytics, and nothing is sent to us. Read the privacy policy.
