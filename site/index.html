Copy, download and share
from X, in one click

A Chrome extension that adds small, quiet buttons to X/Twitter: copy photos, save videos, and turn any post into a clean image card.

Chrome Web Store — coming soon Free. No account, no analytics, no developer server.

Copy photos

Hover a photo, or open it full screen, and click copy. You get the original-size image on your clipboard.

Download videos & GIFs

Hover a video or GIF and click download to save the highest-quality MP4 to your Downloads folder.

Share posts as images

Turn a post into an image card: full text, uncropped media, quoted post, link preview, counts, date and link — drawn in your X theme.

Optional extras

  • Like and/or Bookmark a post automatically after you copy or share it.
  • Expand "Show more" on long posts in your timeline as they appear.
  • If you've pressed X's "Show translation", the card uses that translation.

All extras are off by default. Click the toolbar icon to turn them on.

Private by design

Tweetkit works entirely in your browser. It only talks to X's own servers, the same way the X page does, and never sends your data anywhere else. Read the privacy policy.
